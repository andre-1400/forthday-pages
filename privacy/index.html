Forthday Privacy Policy

Policy version: FD-ONLINE-2026-11-09-v2 · November 9, 2026.

Forthday is operated by Andrey Bytkin, based in Spain. Andrey Bytkin is responsible for the personal data handled through Forthday. For privacy questions, requests or concerns, email andrebytkin@gmail.com.

This policy explains the Forthday iPhone/iPad app, its optional account and social features, Forthday Pro, and these privacy and support pages. Apple and the other providers named below also explain their own processing in their privacy notices.

Current availability

Core local focusing is available at any age without an account. You need to be at least 14 to create a Forthday account. Where self-service signup is available, users 18 and older can enter their age and continue with Apple. Self-service signup for ages 14 to 17 is unavailable. These users may need an additional check or permission and can keep studying without an account. Signing in does not turn on private saving, Friends, study parties, social notifications or purchases. Each has its own permissions and availability.

The essentials

  • You can use the core solo focus tools without creating a Forthday account.
  • Sign in with Apple is the account route. An account does not start a trial or payment. Private saving, Friends, parties, social notifications and new Pro purchases have separate access and choices.
  • Your full birthday and Screen Time app selections stay on your device. We use age in years briefly to check new signup and keep only a broad age band for completed signup.
  • Where Apple requires an age check, the app uses the outcome for account access. A separate review can use the limited information described below.
  • Optional friend progress and studying-status sharing starts off. Private notes are excluded. Party participation and photos have separate rules.
  • Forthday does not include advertising, advertising tracking, a third-party analytics SDK, or a direct external AI integration for your focus content. Provider operations and subprocessors are described below.
  • You can request access, correction or deletion at the contact email above. In-app account deletion and data export are also available.

Information on your device

Forthday stores the answers and content you enter, such as a name or nickname, birthday, interests, goals, motivations, deadlines, routine preferences, focus subjects and notes. It records focus sessions, including mode, dates, duration, breaks and completion or early ending, and uses these records for progress views.

If you use Pro planning, local content can include availability windows, subject allocations, planned sessions, recovery decisions, outcome notes, forecasts, reviews and private party templates. Atlas can store selected city stops, virtual routes, country journals and progress. Choosing a city represents a virtual journey, not evidence of your physical location.

Your birthday, legacy age value, active timer, unfinished drafts, prepared-session pointer, and selected Screen Time apps/sites are excluded from the private cloud snapshot sent by the current app. Some appearance and system preferences also remain local. The main focus documents are protected in the app container and excluded from device backups by the app; this is not a promise about every item iOS may retain in settings, Keychain or system services.

Local focusing does not create an anonymous Forthday cloud account. Using Apple-backed features such as Maps search, notification delivery or purchases can still involve Apple without a Forthday cloud account.

In the developer's private Debug build, administrator-only Preview Account holds separate sample history, fictional profiles and test Pro without creating a purchase. Samples do not sync or become real friend progress. Personal Account uses real local records and StoreKit ownership, subject to the same online restrictions. Migration preserves earlier administrator setup in protected device-local archives before creating the separate Personal setup; these are not customer database exports. Account deletion's local cleanup includes both administrator spaces and their migration archives. Ordinary users and Release/TestFlight do not receive this test mode.

Sign in with Apple and cloud saving

When you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple provides an identifier and the real or relay email address you choose to share. The app requests email. Your Apple password and Apple’s full-name field are outside this request. The name or nickname you type into Forthday is separate. Apple credentials and one-time codes are processed to authenticate your account. Session credentials are kept in the device Keychain and used with Supabase, our authentication provider.

Before new account signup, Forthday sends the age in whole years you enter to check eligibility. The app and service use the exact answer for the check and do not store it as account data. A completed signup links a broad age band, decision date and policy version to the account. Temporary signup records use a short-lived random code to tie the check to the matching Apple signup. Where Apple says an age check is required, Forthday can ask for a range on the device and send a limited outcome, such as a match, mismatch or unavailable result. The server does not receive the range bounds in this flow. A qualifying device-check outcome may be recorded with the short-lived signup record. Returning sign-in does not repeat the signup age question.

Private saving for a new account is an optional choice. Where available, it saves numeric daily and weekly goals, timer preferences and saved solo focus times across your devices. A saved session can contain its start and end, timing mode, focused duration and intervals, breaks, completion or early-ending status, and a record identifier. Subject and project names, intentions, reflections, plans, private Atlas routes and active timers are outside this private-saving format.

Existing accounts may already retain an earlier private cloud copy of saved setup/preferences, subjects, goals, reasons, deadlines, completed session history, milestones, personal plans, outcomes, reviews, Atlas routes/journals and private templates. Returning users can retrieve available saved data. A new cloud space can offer a choice about adopting supported guest progress. Account-specific caches support offline use. Queued local changes remain on the device for retry. Signing in alone does not mean an upload succeeded.

Sign-out is not cloud deletion. It returns you to a guest space and can leave the signed-out account's protected local cache on that device for offline return. Active timers are not transferred live to another device. A failed connection may delay saving, restoration, sharing changes or deletion; the app should not report a failed operation as completed.

If you separately submit answers or request review, Forthday can retain the selected country of residence, necessary state/province, age-band bounds, consent choice, declared signal source, policy version/revision, required-check/review/withdrawal status and timestamps. These are account-linked records and are kept out of your public profile. Purpose-specific permission records can include a verification reference and expiry. A supported initial guardian-verification route is not yet available. A client age or guardian signal is not trusted verification of guardian permission. The app does not upload a full birthday, request GPS or collect identity documents for this process. Merely opening the local reader sends none of these answers.

Friends, profiles and sharing

When social access is available, Forthday stores your chosen nickname/tag, discovery choices, requests, friendships, blocks and sharing preferences. This social graph is the app's contacts data; Forthday does not import your device address book. Enabled exact-tag discovery reveals only the identity needed for a request, not private notes, photo or activity statistics. Requests require acceptance; current server restrictions still apply.

The implemented sharing flow asks for one optional, initially unticked choice before any covered progress is shared. Sharing also requires the current policy notice and applicable eligibility checks to be ready; implementation alone does not enable disclosure. Signing in, finishing onboarding, buying Pro or accepting a friend does not by itself give that permission. You can continue using private Forthday tools without sharing. The choice covers accepted, unblocked friends, including friends you accept later, and the retained account history described below.

  • Individual saved focus/study entries: the subject name or reviewed shared label and actual focused duration only. Earlier retained saved account sessions and new saved-ended sessions while sharing is on are included, including saved sessions ended early. Entries contain no session date, start/end timestamp, location, route or time-based order. Active/prepared sessions, deleted records, isolated samples and unadopted guest history are not included.
  • Progress totals: current-week focus/study time, session and active-day totals; daily totals/counts for the last 84 days; lifetime totals, average/longest duration and earned preset badges; current numeric daily/weekly targets and progress. Study time is not counted twice. Dated totals do not link to individual subject entries. Some aggregate days use UTC and may differ from your local-day view.
  • Chosen virtual Atlas passport: one selected curated route and up to three selected earned country stamps. Stamps can reflect earlier progress. This is virtual progress, not physical travel or GPS; private city routes, journals, travel dates and companion identities are not shared.
  • Minimal studying status: whether you are currently studying. No live subject, start time, elapsed/countdown timer, last-seen history, party identity or physical location is shown through this status. It is based on a recent report, not verified attention, and becomes quiet when that report is stale.

Private notes are not shared, including milestone/reflection text, private reasons, intentions, reviews and plans. Email, birthday, pause timelines and private session/run identifiers are not friend-progress fields. A nickname/tag labels the shared profile. Photo permissions and an explicitly joined party are separate, not enabled by this progress choice.

Before sharing, review your existing subject labels and history. The sharing controls provide shared-label editing and subject-entry hiding without changing the private original. Sensitive or other people's information needs additional protection; a general sharing choice is not permission to publish every possible sensitive label. Known affected entries are held or kept private until appropriately resolved. Do not put private notes into a shared subject name.

Removing exact session times does not prevent every timing inference. Daily totals reveal activity days, studying status reveals activity now, and a distinctive duration can sometimes be matched to a daily total. Friends may also copy or capture what they see.

Use the Sharing controls to turn progress sharing off or change supported label/subject choices. A successful server acknowledgement stops access to covered earlier and future progress and live status; it does not require deleting your private account/history. Removing or blocking a friend also restricts subsequent access. An offline or failed request may remain pending rather than having stopped remote access. Limited cached/previously rendered information and copies a friend already made cannot all be recalled.

Open the app’s progress sharing controls for sharing choices and subject-label review. My profile sharing/activity controls also lead there. Withdrawal can remain visibly pending offline and is retried for that account. Reading this policy or viewing a Preview profile does not enable sharing.

Photos

If you choose a profile photo through Apple's photo picker, Forthday receives the image you select rather than access to your whole photo library. It prepares a smaller JPEG locally, removes the original EXIF/GPS metadata, and uploads the prepared copy when you save it. The original photo is not uploaded by this process.

Photos are stored in private account storage. Shared photos can be viewed by accepted, unblocked friends when the relevant permissions and safety checks apply. Profile photos are not shown to strangers in tag search or to study-party participants who are not your accepted friends. You can replace or remove the photo. Downloaded profile photos use a bounded memory cache rather than an app-managed permanent photo folder. Information visible within the image itself remains visible despite metadata removal.

Shared goals, study parties and reports

Forthday stores invitations, membership, shared labels, targets, selected shared contributions, party timing, readiness, host choices, participation/foreground updates, breaks and checked-time receipts so that these features work. Other accepted participants can see shared goal/party information and relevant roster/status. Private reasons, notes and local exception choices are not copied into that view. Subject names may separately appear in the limited friend-progress entries only under the relevant choice and accepted-friend rules. Joining a party shares participation with its members even when a separate friend-status badge is off; this is a distinct participation flow.

Contributing saved time to a shared goal or Together route requires your selection. A Studied together profile entry requires the relevant separate choices and qualifying overlap; it shows limited recorded information rather than other people's private subject details. You can withdraw shared contributions or entries through the available controls. A private recurring party template does not automatically invite people or start sessions.

The server checks recorded intervals, not whether you were attentive, learned something, or achieved a real-world result. Leaving or archiving a party does not erase another person's own recorded time. Removing a journey or hiding a milestone/tree entry also does not necessarily delete its underlying session history.

When you report someone, we store account references, a fixed report reason and a timestamp for safety handling. Reporting/blocking affects access and relationships. If you separately send evidence by email, that message and any attachments are also handled as support information. No free-text friend messaging, public posts or public comment feed is provided by the current app. Names, photos and shared labels are still user-provided content.

Notifications and Live Activities

With your device permission and relevant preferences, Forthday can schedule local timer-completion alerts and send social notifications through Apple's Push Notification service. For signed-in social alerts, we store an installation identifier, push token, delivery environment, account/session association, preferences, event identifiers and delivery/retry status. These are used for delivery and cleanup, not advertising.

Social payloads use generic text and routing identifiers. They do not include private subject, intention, birthday, photo or precise route information. Friend-activity alerts also depend on the sender's status-sharing choice and the recipient's separate opt-in. Delivery is controlled by Apple and device settings and is not guaranteed.

You can change notification choices in Forthday and device Settings. Signing out detaches the installation where the connection allows, and server sessions/preferences also restrict delivery. Local Live Activities are separately controlled and show a generic timer/progress presentation without private focus or party names. Daily motivational and Atlas reminder schedules are not part of the current implementation described here.

Screen Time and app blocking

Screen Time blocking uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks with your authorization. Opaque app/site selections, allowed-app reasons and blocking state are stored locally, including in a protected app/extension shared container. They are not uploaded for friend statistics, advertising or external AI evaluation. Social statistics come from Forthday's own session records, not a report of your device's Screen Time use.

The operating system controls supported restrictions and permissions. You can end your own session, including in an emergency, and revoke Screen Time authorization. Forthday does not provide an inescapable device lockdown.

Atlas city search and Apple services

Searching for cities and requesting driving estimates use Apple Maps. Your query and selected endpoints are sent to Apple to obtain results. Forthday does not request device GPS/location access for Atlas. Selected places and their coordinates can be saved in your local route. Some existing accounts have Atlas routes in an earlier private cloud snapshot.

The globe and saved routes can work offline. Flight times are planning estimates; driving estimates depend on Apple's available routes. Virtual distance and forecasts do not establish physical travel or verified attention. Read Apple Maps & Privacy for Apple's own handling.

Pro purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes Pro subscriptions, introductory offers, billing and payment details. Forthday does not ask for or receive your payment-card number. It reads verified StoreKit transaction information, including product, ownership, environment and relevant subscription status, to provide access and restoration. It supplies your signed-in Forthday account UUID to Apple as an app-account token when starting a new purchase, associating that transaction with an app account.

The current implementation does not send StoreKit entitlement state to a custom Forthday subscription server or store it in the private focus snapshot. Apple may provide the developer with transaction, sales and financial reporting through its services. The App Store Apple Account controls subscription ownership and may differ from the Apple identity used to sign in to Forthday. Restore and previously verified ownership remain available while signed out of Forthday.

The purchase screen displays the actual localized price and any eligible introductory offer. Signing in or viewing a Pro preview does not begin a trial. Deleting a Forthday account or deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Use the in-app Manage subscription control or Apple subscription settings. Apple's standard app licence applies unless a different applicable agreement is provided.

Technical information, support and this website

Supabase authentication can retain IP addresses, user-agent details and security audit records. Provider infrastructure logs can be accessible to the operator for service operation, security and troubleshooting. The app/backend code does not deliberately log Apple authorization codes, private keys or raw authentication tokens. This does not mean that no operational metadata exists.

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple may supply crash information, tester usage and the feedback you choose to send, subject to its beta-service settings. We use relevant reports to investigate defects and improve reliability. Forthday does not currently contain an advertising or third-party product-analytics SDK. There is no external AI service for evaluating your notes or focus reasons.

If you email us, we receive your address, message and chosen attachments. We use Gmail/Google to receive and manage that correspondence. Please send only what is needed; do not email passwords, verification codes, private keys, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive notes.

The public Forthday privacy and support website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement for its handling. The bundled in-app copy is read locally and does not contact GitHub merely to display the text.

These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded third-party media, cookies or browser-storage features of their own. Reading a bundled copy requires no network request. Selecting an external link or opening an email application involves the selected provider; a mail link does not itself send a message or submit a support form.

Why we use information and who receives it

Where European/UK data-protection law applies, Forthday uses necessary account and private-sync information to provide the service requested under the applicable service agreement; proportionate security, abuse handling, troubleshooting and support rely on legitimate interests, subject to users' rights and additional protection for children; legal obligations apply to records we must keep. Optional friend-progress disclosure uses a separate affirmative choice. Eligibility information is used to apply age, consent and platform requirements and protect users; the appropriate legal basis depends on the particular requirement. Consent is obtained where required and can be withdrawn without affecting prior lawful processing. A platform permission or a single checkbox does not authorise unrelated processing or establish a child's contractual capacity.

Each online feature depends on the applicable access and permission rules. A feature can remain unavailable even after successful signup. Sensitive information about you or another person can require additional protection even when entered in a free-text field. Keep unnecessary sensitive information out of names, shared labels and support messages. An ordinary account or sharing choice does not automatically authorise sensitive-data processing.

Our main service providers are Supabase for authentication/database/storage/server functions, Apple for authentication/purchases/push/Maps/system capabilities, Google/Gmail for support email, and GitHub Pages for this public website. Supabase uses infrastructure and other subprocessors described in its current list. These providers have different roles for service processing and their own account/security activities. Authorized operator/provider personnel may access information as necessary for their roles. Private cloud content is not end-to-end encrypted against all operator/provider access.

We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-company targeted advertising. Other disclosures may be necessary to comply with law, protect people or the service, or handle a genuine business transfer under appropriate safeguards and notice. We do not publish private focus content as part of a transfer or make it available for unrestricted use.

How long information stays

Your account's private setup, focus history and planning content are kept while your account exists unless an available deletion control removes them. There is currently no automatic inactive-account deletion schedule. Eligibility answers, permission records and consent/review event history are also account-linked and removed with account deletion under the current schema. Permission expiry or withdrawal restricts access; it is not automatic erasure of that history. No separate eligibility-event purge is currently implemented. Sharing less information, signing out, archiving a party, hiding a tree branch or removing a journey is not the same as erasing account history.

You can remove or replace your current profile photo. Successful account deletion removes the active cloud account, its owned profile/session data and associated records through the deletion process, including the current avatar object. Other people's independently saved time can remain. A necessary safety or progress report may remain until its case-retention deadline with the deleted account's identifying references removed; this is not a promise of irreversible anonymity. Narrow records kept for an active case or legal obligation follow the applicable documented exception.

The notification cleanup code removes events once they have been expired for more than seven days and inactive installation records after 90 days, when the worker runs. Related jobs are removed with their events/installations. Visible focus status becomes stale after about 45 seconds without refresh, although an expired row may remain until updated, disabled or its session/account is deleted.

Local account caches can remain on a signed-out or disconnected device. Forthday cannot remotely erase every disconnected copy, user export or screenshot. Apple's billing records and provider-controlled records follow their own applicable requirements.

Our operator procedure is to delete ordinary support correspondence promptly once the conversation has ended and the issue is resolved. We keep only specific information still needed for an active safety/security matter, a legal obligation or a claim, with access and retention limited to that need. A Gmail Delete action can leave a message in Trash for up to 30 days unless it is permanently deleted earlier. Deletion from our mailbox does not mean immediate destruction of Google's managed backups or other people's copies.

We do not make personal manual database exports/backups or use customer data for unrelated purposes. This does not remove provider-managed operational logs, backups or legally necessary records, or prevent you from exporting your own focus data.

Closed abuse reports and progress-label reports become due for deletion 12 UTC calendar months after actual closure. Daily cleanup is scheduled for 03:15 UTC and removes eligible records when it runs; this is not a promise of deletion at the exact anniversary instant. Open cases are reviewed rather than assigned a fictional closure date. Reopening removes the deadline until actual reclosure. Necessary safety/legal holds require a reason, review date and expiry; each reviewed hold is limited to 90 days and any further hold needs renewed justification. Account deletion removes the deleted account's identity references while necessary minimized case context follows this lifecycle. Cleanup logs contain counts and timestamps, not report content, and are removed after 13 months when cleanup runs.

Where Spanish law requires data to be reserved or blocked after deletion for a liability period, the necessary data must be kept separately restricted for the permitted legal purpose and then destroyed when that period ends; it is not available for ordinary or unrelated use.

We retain only information still needed for the stated purpose or a lawful obligation, and review remaining support, safety, security and backup records accordingly. We will not describe a dashboard log-access window as a guarantee that every provider record has already been erased.

Your choices, rights and deletion

You can use guest solo tools, choose whether to adopt guest data into an account, edit supported profile/planning information, change sharing and notification settings, remove your photo, withdraw shared contributions, remove/block users, export your focus data and initiate account deletion. Uninstalling the app does not delete cloud data or cancel billing; iOS Offload App can retain local documents.

For account deletion, open Settings → Apple account → Delete account. Confirm your identity as requested. The normal flow uses fresh Apple confirmation to revoke app authorization; an explicit manual-revocation fallback explains any remaining Apple-access removal. The app reports errors rather than claiming incomplete deletion succeeded. If you cannot access the app or the process fails, email andrebytkin@gmail.com for help. We may need proportionate verification, but will not ask for your Apple password.

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and consent withdrawal. Contact us to exercise them, including for information not included in the in-app export. We respond within the applicable legal period; GDPR requests are generally answered within one month, subject to lawful extensions or exceptions. You may complain to your competent data-protection authority, including in the EEA country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred, or to the UK Information Commissioner's Office where applicable. In Spain, the supervisory authority is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos. You do not have to contact us first to make a lawful complaint.

Planning suggestions and forecasts use your selected settings and recorded history. You remain in control; Forthday does not make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you. This policy does not limit consumer or privacy rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

Children and younger users

Local focusing is available at any age. You need to be at least 14 to create a Forthday account. Self-service signup starts at 18 where available. Ages 14 to 17 may need additional checks or permission and can keep using the app offline while self-service signup is unavailable. The App Store content rating is separate from account eligibility.

Forthday does not currently offer a guardian-managed signup route. Missing or conflicting age information does not establish adulthood. Existing data retrieval, deletion, subscription ownership and Restore, and emergency session ending stay available through their existing controls.

Apple sign-in, a download or purchase approval does not establish parental data consent. Shared labels, photos, identifiers, activity and party participation can contain personal data. Access to these features requires its own permissions and any required checks.

If you believe a child has provided information in circumstances requiring parental consent or other safeguards, contact andrebytkin@gmail.com. We will assess the situation and take required steps. Do not send identity documents or sensitive information by ordinary email unless an appropriate secure process has been agreed.
