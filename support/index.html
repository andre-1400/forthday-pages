Pro, trials and billing

Open Settings → Forthday Pro to see your actual offer and available purchase/Restore controls. An owner preview or test label does not establish a paid subscription or begin a real trial. If the app says purchases are not open or the product is unavailable, it has not started a payment merely by showing that message.

In the developer's private Debug build, Preview Account is an administrator-only test space with sample progress and test Pro. It replays onboarding when reopened. Personal Account starts with real, separate progress and saves completed onboarding once; its Pro status follows genuine Apple ownership and Restore.
