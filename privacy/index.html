Privacy at a glance

  • Study without an account. You can use solo study tools on your device at any age. Signing in and saving across devices are separate choices.
  • Use a tag to connect. If you allow exact-tag discovery, an eligible account that enters your whole tag can see that tag. An optional nickname is visible to accepted friends only. Forthday has no profile photo feature or general live study status.
  • Choose what you share. Study parties are invite-only and display tags. A party receipt sends timing data to verify the shared session. Friend Highlights and contributions to shared goals or Atlas routes are separate choices.
  • Control your information. You can turn off optional sharing, block or report another account, export your focus data, and request deletion. Forthday has no ads or cross-company ad tracking.

Study on your device

Forthday keeps your study setup, goals, preferences, plans, subjects, notes, timers and focus history on your device. What is stored depends on what you use. For example, your local records can include answers from setup, a name you enter, session times and breaks, and the virtual places you choose in Atlas. A birthday saved by an older version may still be on your device. Forthday uses these details to show your progress and offer planning suggestions. Using the solo tools creates no cloud account.

When you enable Screen Time controls, Apple’s frameworks handle the restrictions. Your app and website selections and blocking settings stay on your device, including in the protected area used by the app’s extension. You can end your own session and revoke authorization. When you search for a city or request a driving estimate, Apple Maps receives the query or route endpoints. Forthday does not ask for your GPS location for Atlas. Atlas places and stamps represent in-app journeys. Apple Maps and Privacy.

Account, age and private saving

Where new accounts are available, choose Under 13 or 13 or older. If you are under 13, you can continue studying on your device and the ordinary signup flow stops before account creation. For this signup we ask for an age group, without asking for a birth date or country of residence or using IP location to decide eligibility. Where needed, Apple can require a separate age-related check or limit sign-in. The age answer is self-reported. Access can depend on local and platform requirements. A download, Apple sign-in or purchase approval does not establish parental consent for data processing.

If you create an account, Apple provides an identifier and the real or relay email address you choose to share. Forthday asks for email, without requesting your Apple password or Apple's full-name field. Supabase processes authentication. Device session credentials are kept in the Keychain. We may record your eligibility outcome, decision date and rule, and the version and time of your Free Account Terms agreement. The required account information lets us authenticate and provide the account. If you choose not to provide it, you can continue with local study. Previous signup routes can have other age bands or limited eligibility records. Returning sign-in does not ask you to repeat the ordinary signup question.

Optional private saving starts off. If you choose it, the current format saves numerical goals, timer preferences and completed or ended solo focus records, such as times, focused intervals, mode and completion status. It leaves out your subject and project names, notes, plans, private routes and active timer. Some earlier accounts may already have an older private cloud copy with broader study setup, plans, session history, Atlas data or templates. Signing in does not automatically upload previous guest history. If the app offers to bring it over, it asks first. Signing out returns you to guest mode and can leave a protected local cache for later use.

Friends, parties and sharing

Tags and friend requests

If you set up Friends, we keep your tag, any nickname you provide, discovery choice, requests, relationships and blocks. Exact-tag discovery starts off for new accounts. If you turn it on, an eligible signed-in person who enters the complete tag can confirm the tag and request a connection. Search and pending requests show tags in place of nicknames. Only accepted friends can see the nickname you choose to provide. Turning discovery off stops new lookups and requests through discovery. Existing friends remain connected until removed or blocked. Forthday does not import your device contacts.

Invite-only study parties

Parties use preset names. Invitees and members see the tags and invitations needed for the party. During an active party, authorized participants see limited readiness, shared timer, break and session status. The host can receive break requests. A party participant does not receive your general study status or a running total of your personal focus time. After the party, the shared view does not show other members’ individual session history.

To validate a party receipt, Forthday sends the issued record and session identifiers, timer mode, start and end times, focused intervals and completion status to the server. It can also send the times of personal breaks recorded in the party. The upload leaves out private titles, subjects, notes and reflections. A party receipt can be saved even when private solo saving is off. A receipt measures the timer and cannot determine how much you learned or whether you paid attention.

Highlights and shared contributions

Friend Highlights starts off and has its own control. If you enable it, accepted, unblocked friends can see your study streak, preset achievement badges and up to three virtual Atlas country stamps you select. A badge may reveal the milestone it represents. Highlights do not show exact hours, notes, subjects, location or full session history. You can stop sharing in Settings. An offline change takes effect for other people when the server confirms it.

Sharing a contribution to a goal or a Together Atlas route is another action you choose. To record that contribution, Forthday uploads a separate timing record. It leaves out private titles, subjects, notes and reflections. The relevant people can see the selected contribution and the information needed for that shared goal or route. You can withdraw your contributions with the app’s controls. Party membership or enabling Highlights does not share your entire study record. Removing a friend or blocking them stops access through that relationship. We cannot retrieve copies other people already made.

Safety and alerts

Use the available block and report controls on tags, profiles and supported shared items. Reports can include account or content references, a selected reason and timing so we can review them. If you send additional information to Support, it is handled as support correspondence. Use a tag or nickname that does not reveal private details about yourself or someone else.

Optional social alerts use Apple Push Notification service. To deliver them, we use an installation identifier, device push token, account association, preferences, routing identifiers and delivery or retry status. Alerts use generic text for events such as a request or invitation. You can change your choice in Forthday and in device settings. Local timer alerts and Live Activities have separate controls.

Why we use data and who helps

  • To provide the service you request. Where a valid agreement applies, we use the data objectively needed for your free account and the features you request, such as private saving, a friend request or an invited party. Agreeing to the Free Account Terms does not turn on optional sharing or start a purchase.
  • To honor optional choices. Exact-tag discovery, Friend Highlights and any other optional disclosure are enabled only through the relevant choice and where a lawful basis applies. Where we rely on consent, you can refuse or withdraw it without closing your account. A device permission controls device access and is separate from consent to other uses.
  • To run and protect the service. We use limited eligibility, request, report, security and troubleshooting information where necessary for a specific legal requirement or for a justified legitimate interest that takes your rights, including children’s rights, into account. Provider logs may include IP address, approximate region derived from IP, device/software details, account or session references, request timing and errors. We use these logs to operate and protect the service and diagnose issues.

Service providers. Supabase provides authentication, cloud storage, database and server services. The project is hosted in London, United Kingdom. Other provider operations can take place elsewhere. Apple provides sign-in, Maps, push notifications and purchases. Google/Gmail handles the support mailbox. GitHub Pages hosts this website and processes visitor IP addresses for security. The website itself adds no advertising, analytics scripts or cookies. The bundled copy reads offline and contacts no website merely to display the text. Providers handle data under applicable privacy terms and agreements requiring protection appropriate to their role. Supabase subprocessors · GitHub Pages data collection.

We use applicable transfer safeguards such as recognized adequacy decisions or contractual protections. Supabase's Data Processing Addendum includes international-transfer provisions. You can ask us for information about the safeguards and an available copy, subject to necessary confidentiality protections. Authorized personnel may access data as needed for their roles. Private cloud storage is access-controlled and is not end-to-end encrypted against all provider or operator access. We may disclose limited information when required by law or where necessary for an authorized safety, security or legal matter. We do not sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising.

Purchases. Apple handles billing and payment details. Forthday reads verified StoreKit entitlement information on your device to provide Pro or Restore. Starting an eligible signed-in purchase can send your Forthday account identifier to Apple as an app-account token. We do not send StoreKit entitlement records to a Forthday subscription server. The operator reviews Apple’s aggregate sales and income totals only. Apple manages subscriptions. Deleting Forthday or its account does not cancel them. Manage Apple subscriptions.

How long we keep information

  • Account and study records. We keep account, agreement, session and choice records while needed for your account or a distinct lawful purpose. No automatic inactive-account deletion schedule applies. Turning off a choice or signing out does not erase history already stored. Account deletion removes the active account and associated owned data, subject to limited legal and safety records. Other people’s own session records can remain.
  • Earlier versions. Earlier test versions used profile photos and could keep broader shared labels and study data. We removed the former profile photo files from active storage. Provider-managed backups, older account data and downloaded copies may have separate retention periods.
  • Reports. Closed abuse and progress-label reports become due for deletion 12 UTC calendar months after actual closure. Scheduled cleanup removes eligible records when it runs. Open and reopened cases are reviewed. Necessary safety or legal holds are reasoned and reviewed, with a maximum of 90 days per hold and a new justification for any extension. Account deletion removes the deleted account’s identifying references from necessary retained reports. Cleanup logs contain counts and timestamps and are due for removal after 13 months.
  • Notifications and deletion confirmation. Cleanup removes push events expired for more than seven days and inactive installations after 90 days when the worker runs. A temporary recovery receipt can confirm account deletion and Apple access removal if the response was interrupted. Its recovery window is seven days.
  • Support and infrastructure. We delete ordinary support correspondence promptly after resolving the issue. Limited information can remain for an active case, legal duty or claim. A deleted Gmail message can remain in Trash for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted earlier. Provider-controlled logs, backups and Apple billing records follow their applicable requirements. Disconnected devices, user exports and screenshots can retain separate copies.

Earlier eligibility review challenges expire after two days, and scheduled cleanup removes eligible expired challenges and unbound reviews. Limited permission or Apple-withdrawal records may be needed to honor a decision or prevent reactivation. Where Spanish law requires data to be reserved after deletion for a liability period, the necessary information is separately restricted to the permitted legal purpose and destroyed when that period ends. A provider's log-access window does not establish when every backup or operational record is erased.

Your choices and rights

Use Settings to change private saving, discovery, Highlights or notification choices, export your focus records, or select Delete account. Follow the confirmation steps for account deletion and Apple access removal. Signing out, uninstalling or offloading the app is a separate action. To erase guest data, use the local erase option. If you cannot access the app or need help deleting an account, contact Support.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Email andrebytkin@gmail.com if you need help or think an underage person’s information was collected improperly. We may need proportionate verification. Keep passwords, codes and unnecessary private information out of email. GDPR requests are generally answered within one month, subject to lawful exceptions or extensions. You can complain to your competent data protection authority, including Spain’s AEPD where applicable, without first contacting us.

Planning suggestions use your settings and recorded history. You remain in control. Forthday does not make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you.

If we learn that a child’s account was created without meeting an applicable requirement, we restrict affected access and address the data under applicable law. Core local study remains available. We will update this notice as the service changes and provide further notice or seek a new choice where required.
