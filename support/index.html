Forthday Support

Current availability

Forthday is currently in private testing, with local focusing available. New account entry, cloud uploads, real social features, remote social notifications and new purchases are currently restricted while eligibility and privacy prerequisites are completed. Existing-account retrieval, data rights, consent withdrawal, emergency ending and legitimate owned access/Restore remain available by design. A failed or pending operation has not completed; contact support if it does not recover.

For help with Forthday, contact Andrey Bytkin, Spain, at andrebytkin@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone/iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, Forthday version, what you tried and what happened. If useful, attach a screenshot with private details covered. Do not send your Apple password, verification codes, payment-card details, private keys or unnecessary personal notes. This support channel is not an emergency service and does not promise an immediate response.

We delete ordinary support correspondence promptly after the conversation has ended and the issue is resolved, except for specific information needed for an active safety matter, legal obligation or claim. Deleting it from our mailbox does not immediately erase provider-managed backups or other people's copies.

Focus without an account

Core solo focusing remains available as a guest. You can use timers, stopwatch/Pomodoro, ordinary goals and progress without subscribing. Emergency ending remains available during a session, including when offline.

Friends and cloud saving need Sign in with Apple. A new Pro purchase currently also requires Forthday sign-in. Signing in alone starts no trial or payment; the purchase requires a separate action and Apple's confirmation.

Pro, trials and billing

Open Settings → Forthday Pro to see your actual offer and available purchase/Restore controls. Where available, an eligible one-week introductory trial is followed by the localized monthly price displayed by Apple. Not everyone qualifies for a trial. The App Store confirmation contains the applicable terms; no €1.99 follow-up discount is promised.

An owner preview or test label does not establish a paid subscription or begin a real trial. If the app says purchases are not open or the product is unavailable, it has not started a payment merely by showing that message.

In the developer's private Debug build, Preview Account is an administrator-only test space with sample progress and test Pro. It replays onboarding when reopened. Personal Account starts with real, separate progress and saves completed onboarding once; its Pro status follows genuine Apple ownership and Restore. Switching modes does not transfer sample achievements or test Pro into Personal. This Preview/test-Pro mode is unavailable to ordinary accounts and Release/TestFlight. It sends no sample progress or fictional profiles to real friends, and it cannot start purchases or delete the real account. Real deletion belongs to Personal.

To cancel renewal, use Manage subscription in the Pro page or open device Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Forthday Pro. You can also open Apple subscription settings. If you do not want a trial to renew, Apple advises cancelling at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Review any expiry information Apple shows after cancellation. Apple cancellation help.

For a refund request, use Apple's refund service or read Apple's refund instructions. Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions under applicable rules; emailing Forthday does not itself cancel a subscription or submit a refund request.

Restore a purchase or understand Pro access

Choose Restore on the Pro page while using the App Store Apple Account that owns the subscription. Restore and previously verified ownership remain available even when signed out of Forthday. Your App Store billing account can be different from the Apple identity used for Forthday sign-in.

If access appears wrong, check Apple Subscriptions first, try Restore and make sure the device is online. If a purchase is pending or cannot be verified, do not keep starting new purchases: check its status or contact us. Signing out of Forthday does not cancel Apple billing. After expiry, existing saved plans, notes, history and data export remain available; creating or reshaping Pro plans may need an active subscription.

Save, restore and export your focus data

Existing accounts can sign in to retrieve saved information. New cloud uploads are currently restricted, so a local change or successful sign-in does not mean it has synced. Queued local records are preserved for retry. When sync is available, use Settings → Apple account → Sync now; a new cloud space asks whether to adopt guest progress. Resolve setup conflicts deliberately and avoid replacing a saved space with unintended empty data.

Offline changes may need to reconnect before they appear elsewhere. An active timer stays on its current device. Signing out preserves account data and can keep a protected account cache on that device; it is not deletion.

Use Settings → Plan & review → Export my focus data to create a copy of your own focus/planning information. Treat exported files as private. For an access request covering information outside that export, email us. Hidden milestone branches and removed Atlas journeys can retain their recorded session history.

Delete your account or local data

Open Settings → Apple account → Delete account. Review what will be removed and complete the Apple confirmation. The app offers a clearly explained manual Apple-access-removal fallback when needed. If deletion fails, reconnect and retry, or email andrebytkin@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership; do not send your Apple password.

Deleting your Forthday account or removing the app does not cancel Apple billing. Use Apple's subscription-management controls separately. Account deletion remains available without buying Pro or cancelling first.

Deletion removes the current account's active cloud data and local workspace through the deletion process, including the administrator's Preview/Personal spaces and migration archives where applicable. Disconnected devices may retain downloaded copies; other people can retain information they already saw. Necessary safety/progress-report context can remain with the deleted account's identity references removed. Closed cases become due for deletion after 12 UTC calendar months; daily cleanup and limited reviewed safety/legal holds apply. Provider/backup retention is explained in the privacy policy.

For guest data, use available editing/removal controls or delete the app through iOS/iPadOS Settings. Offload App is different and can retain documents. Uninstalling does not request cloud-account deletion, revoke every Apple authorization, or guarantee removal of all platform-managed Keychain information.

Control your profile and activity sharing

Sharing is currently disabled. When available, it starts with one optional unticked choice. Review the preview first: accepted friends, including friends you add later, may see subject names/shared labels and focused durations from retained older and new saved sessions. Individual entries have no date, start/end timestamp, physical location, route or chronological order. Private notes stay private.

The choice also covers dated daily/weekly totals, lifetime statistics/badges, current numeric goals, the chosen virtual Atlas passport and minimal Currently studying status. The status shows no live subject, start/elapsed timer, last-seen history or location. Dated totals/status still reveal some activity timing; hiding session timestamps cannot prevent every inference.

Use Settings → Progress sharing to turn progress sharing off and manage the supported shared-label/subject controls. An acknowledged server withdrawal stops covered earlier/future progress and live-status access without deleting your private history. If offline or an error occurs, wait for acknowledgement rather than assuming remote access has already stopped. Copies already made cannot be recalled. Existing privacy choices must not be silently expanded.

Only a limited discovery identity is visible through enabled exact-tag search. Private reasons, birthday, notes, plans and raw timelines are not friend-progress fields. Photos and explicitly joined parties have separate rules; accepted party participants may have roster/party/photo access even without mutual friendship. The progress choice does not authorise non-friend access to your individual entries or override another participant's choices.

You can remove or block someone from the relevant Friends/profile controls. Use Report and block for abusive behavior. To send extra evidence or a privacy/security concern, email andrebytkin@gmail.com. Include only the relevant tag/party, approximate time and a redacted description. Do not use a public GitHub issue for private account information or a security vulnerability.

Notifications and Live Activities

Use Settings → Notifications → Notification choices in Forthday, and the device's notification Settings, to change alerts. Notification permission is optional. Friend-activity alerts have a separate opt-in. Apple Focus, Scheduled Summary and other device settings can delay or suppress notifications.

Local Live Activities have their own Forthday/device setting. Dismissing one does not end the focus session. Their timers do not guarantee background execution or on-time notification delivery. Daily motivational and Atlas reminder schedules are not currently offered by this implementation.

If app blocking stays on or does not work

Use End session to finish safely and release Forthday's blocking request. If a shield remains, review the app's Screen Time permission under your device's Settings; Settings names can vary by OS version. Revoking Forthday's Screen Time authorization is a recovery option. Do not wait for email support in an emergency.

If blocking is unavailable or permission was declined, solo timing can still work. Blocking affects supported apps with up to three selected exceptions; allowed browsers can browse normally. Essential system controls remain available. No app can promise an inescapable full-device lockdown.

Atlas and planning results

City search and driving estimates need a connection to Apple Maps. Try a more specific city name if results are ambiguous. A missing driving connection is not replaced with an invented road route. Saved routes and the globe can work offline.

Atlas shows virtual travel based on recorded focus. Flight durations and goal forecasts are estimates, not live schedules, navigation, proof of attention or guarantees. Planning can show a shortfall when your target and breaks do not fit the time you made available.

Privacy, younger users and accessibility

Read the privacy policy for data handling, retention, rights and provider information. Email andrebytkin@gmail.com for a privacy request or concern about a younger user's account.

Accounts, Pro and friends remain intended in all 175 selected countries, subject to applicable legal, guardian and Apple requirements. Regional approval and verified guardian/native integration remain incomplete. There is no worldwide 14+ rule. Use Settings → Apple account → Account eligibility for existing-account answers, review or withdrawal, and Continue locally when online access is unavailable. Declined, unavailable or conflicting age evidence does not establish adulthood. An Apple purchase approval or a parent's login is not proof of Forthday data consent.

Forthday provides appearance/motion preferences and native accessibility support. If a control is difficult to use, tell us the device, text size and assistive feature involved. We do not treat build checks as proof that every accessibility feature has been verified.

Forthday uses Apple's standard app licence. A support form or GitHub account is not needed to email us.
